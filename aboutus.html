The Power of Externships and Master Classes!

At Sri Tulasi EdTech PVT LTD, we offer students a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in real-world work environments allowing them to put their learning into practice and explore their career aspirations. Our externship programs prioritize experience over compensation, with a focus on practical exposure rather than financial gain. Through these hands-on experiences, students can confirm whether their chosen field aligns with their passion and skills. Additionally, our interactive master classes break down complex topics, making it easier for learners to grasp today's cutting-edge technologies and excel in their careers.

Mission

"To empower aspiring professionals through high-quality, industry-relevant technology education and hands-on training, fostering innovation, employability, and lifelong learning. At Sri Tulasi EdTech, we are committed to bridging the gap between talent and opportunity by delivering excellence in education, mentorship, and placement support."

Vision

"To be a global leader in technology education, shaping the future of learning through innovation, inclusivity, and excellence. We envision a world where every learner has access to cutting-edge skills, hands-on experiences, and meaningful career opportunities—empowered by the knowledge and confidence they gain at Sri Tulasi EdTech."
